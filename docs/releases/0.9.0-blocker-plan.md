# After 0.9.0 alpha: remaining blocker plan

[0.9.0](0.9.0.md#verified-release-evidence) was published on 2026-10-28 from
`d756b68bc8b44a4da69862214a19ebb25034d1eb`. Its exact-commit CI, matching
packages, selected source/installed checks and six anonymous download hashes
were accepted for the documented clean packed 4K profile. The work below extends
that finite alpha; it is not a pending-publication claim or stable acceptance.
Published 0.8 and earlier evidence stays bound to its original bytes.

The target remains full artist-workflow 1.0 with durable portable `.blend` and
textured/skinned/animated GLB and FBX delivery. **All 29 implementation items and
all 14 stable-release gates are OPEN.** A finite clean-file evidence increment
does not close H01, current pixels, recovery or any full artist requirement.
Use official, unmodified Blender. Windows implementation/testing remains
**deferred and unverified** until functional implementation is complete, then
remains a mandatory stable gate. Do not lower the target to fit this alpha.

## 1. Ordinary large projects and native save — H01, F01, F03, F05, I02

**Finite increment:** prerequisite commit
`6112fdf54e6464bf15ef9473ed9a57eef4650e69` passed the real queued single-clean-4K
fixture with an existing empty sequencer/public channels on official Linux
4.5.14 and 5.2.2. All ten scenario assertions passed on both; the final 0.9
commit and packages later passed their own separately retained qualification.
The profile remains 16,777,216 aggregate pixels, 80 MiB packed bytes and a
256 MiB native-file ceiling. Source pressure qualification is false on both.

**Next implementation:** broaden ordinary startup palettes, line styles,
runtime/Viewer classification, authored sequencer state and production asset
dependencies through reviewed source/effect profiles. Retain canonical queued
admission, pure protected refusal, exact CAS, per-write authority, local expiring
consent and retained effects. Preserve all artist data; no helper deletion,
downscaling, factory-name assumptions or cap bypass may force admission.

**Accepted finite validation:** the final ZIP passed genuine Runtime/TCP
preview/start/poll and product-spawned fresh readers on both builds. The 5.2
installed run also measured a bounded low-memory admission refusal; 4.5 did not.
Neither proves successful pressure saving or full memory recovery.

**Next validation:** broaden representative artist-project coverage. Retain the complete
save-mode, denial, stale/manual-change, cancel/revoke/disconnect, reader-failure,
protected/history/retained-artifact cases. Independently compare full declared
state, all pixels and packed bytes. Complete a safely bounded actual pressure
run or keep it explicitly unqualified; never lower the 8 GiB available-memory
or 2 GiB free-disk floors to manufacture success. Then extend to ordinary large
artist projects and broadened dependencies under their own native/install gates.

## 2. Current pixels, paired sizing and original-ID recovery — A04, F03, I02

convert GENERATED images or turn a separate CURRENT_PIXELS helper into durable
original-ID portability. Existing synchronous helper limits and larger job
profiles remain distinct. Unpublished controllers, graph panels and source

release's limits. A separately reviewed 640 MiB packed / 1 GiB saved-file sizing

Keep complete image hashes and dimensions, visible-cgroup-ancestor memory checks,
folder pinning and streamed full-file hashes. Resource observations are not
reservations, and a fixed disk floor does not promise every ceiling-size staged
pair will fit; late failures must retain truthful partial-artifact effects.

**Next validation:** prove complete coordinate-unique Float32 witnesses, alpha,
color roles, source identity, packed bytes and protected consumers. Cover byte
and float sRGB/linear/Non-Color contracts, refusing unsupported associations.
Before helper mutation, independently reopen/fsync create-only old-packed,
current-EXR and manifest backups. Prove forward packing and paired reverse of
old packed bytes plus edited dirty pixels on the same original ID; immediate
and three-deferred-turn checks; fresh-reader edited original pixels before any
restore. Use unchanged 300-second phases, at least 8 GiB observed 4K headroom,
